# Blockhaus UX profile

Aesthetic: Bauhaus poster, Mondrian frames, enamel tiles. Apple Native on motion:
short and purposeful, up to 300ms when it explains what changed.

## Motion

The tokens live in `app/src/design/system.ts`.

**Durations:** `dur.micro` 100, `dur.fast` 150, `dur.base` 200, `dur.slow` 300.

**Interface** (dialogs, cards, menus, buttons, settings, the poster wall):
- ease-out to enter (`enter`), ease-in to leave (`exit`);
- dialogs and cards arrive with `rise` (fade up 8pt, 300ms) and leave with `sink` (150ms);
- no overshoot, no zoom from nothing, no decorative loops;
- only transform and opacity move.

**The board** (and what speaks its language: the track, the tray, the piece strip,
the lesson formula) is a game, and it is the documented exception:
- `hit` (50ms) is the instant of contact before a settle;
- `pop` (300ms, back 2.2): a tile, square or number kicked up in scale coming back to rest;
- `rest` (back 1.5): travel that stops, such as the board meeting a wall, a pushed piece
  riding in, a lifted tile dropping into its socket;
- the milestone show and the chain's waves keep their own named beats in `game.tsx`
  (`STEP`, `ECHO_MS`, `FLIP`, `FLY_HOME`, `FLASH_MS`, `RIPPLE_MS`, `SCATTER_MS`), because
  they are timed to the sounds of the same events;
- the lesson's demo hand and the stuck hint repeat because they teach, not to decorate.

Animation and sound land together. Every animation follows the system's reduced-motion setting.
